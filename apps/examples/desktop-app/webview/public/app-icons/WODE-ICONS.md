# WODE pixel app icons

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Original generated images remain in the local generated-images folder; production PNGs are stored alongside this document, with native copies in src-tauri/icons/app and padded macOS copies in src-tauri/icons/app/macos.

Shared prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished square WODE desktop app icon, no text or lettering. Genuine crisp chunky pixel art rendered on a consistent 64x64 pixel grid, nearest-neighbor aesthetic, carefully shaded arcade game sprite, strong dark pixel outline, small specular pixel highlights. A single oversized central gambling object on a warm charcoal rounded-square tile, generous safe margin, attractive polished readable silhouette at 48px. Flat frontal three-quarter view. No robot, no Cline logo, no smooth vector art, no blurry edges.

Individual subjects appended to the shared prompt:

- `lucky-dice.png`: Two overlapping ivory dice, one showing five pips, one showing three; cobalt blue and violet accent pixels, two tiny gold sparkle pixels.
- `ace-high.png`: Two fanned ivory playing cards, front card showing a large crimson heart and small corner heart symbols, rear card blue patterned back; a tiny golden sparkle. No ranks or letters.
- `casino-chip.png`: An emerald green poker chip with ivory segmented rim and golden center bearing a tiny pixel dice symbol; bright mint shading.
- `jackpot.png`: A red and gold miniature slot machine with three golden cherry symbols on cream reel windows, a chunky red side lever and coin below; rich amber highlights. No numbers.

Production PNGs use 256x256 nearest-neighbor sizing. Lucky Dice also supplies the bundled WODE PNG and Windows ICO. Existing stored icon choices migrate to these new options.
